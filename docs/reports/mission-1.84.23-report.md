## Two families

| family | refusals | field | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GENUINE_OUTPUT_SUPPORT_FAILURE_UNDER_CURRENT_POLICY` | 1 | `candidate_intervention_class` | a concept (*market*) that no supplied statement supports; under the current policy the answer is at fault, and the policy is kept |
| `GENERATION_SURFACE_FORM_MISALIGNMENT` | 6 | `recommended_next_evidence` | each item opens as an instruction (*Identify, Obtain, Observe, Determine*), which the gate does not read as a request; one also carries a promotion word |

The first family is not something a prompt can excuse: the field is a supported assertion, and the
word was not supplied. What a prompt can do is say so, in the terms the gate applies. The second family
is a surface mismatch: the gate reads a request only when it opens with the evidence it names, and
prompt v1.5.0 said what an item does without saying what it looks like.

## Why the prompt and not the gate

Option A, a versioned prompt-side policy, was feasible with no architectural blocker, so gate v1.4.0 was
not touched. Its implementation files (the inflection, support-origin, assertion-audit, v1.3.0 gate,
schema and v1.4.0 gate modules) are byte-identical, and a probe case shows a single comment in one of
them refused.

The policy, `second-opportunity-generation-surface-policy@1.0.0` with renderer
`generation-surface-renderer@1.0.0`, is rendered from first-class objects the gate already owns: the
second-opportunity field policy, the field roles (`NAME_THE_CLASS_ONLY`), the three support channels and
the census rule `NOTHING_PROMOTED_TO_A_CONCLUSION`. It adds only what a model needs to write the forms:

- three canonical request heads, `Evidence of`, `Observation of`, `Identification of`;
- six neutral constructions for leaving a result open (`whether`, `the existence of`, `the extent of`,
  `the identity of`, `the frequency of`, `the relationship between`);
- two imperative contrasts over a placeholder `X`, never over a real subject.

It imports no private name from the gate and reads no file. At construction it refuses a head not shaped
as one capitalised word followed by `of`, and gate 99 then proves every head against the frozen gate,
because a shape check cannot tell a noun from a verb. The renderer raises on a missing rule or on any
digit in the block.

The block has two sections. **Fields that assert** lists the five supported-assertion fields and the
three places their words may come from, says that general knowledge is not support, and states that
`candidate_intervention_class` names a class that is itself asserted as supported: `NAME_THE_CLASS_ONLY`
is not free vocabulary, and where a richer label would need an unsupplied concept, a more neutral
grounded class is named. **What a request for evidence looks like** gives the noun-phrase form with the
three heads, forbids an item opening as an instruction (with the two contrasts), asks for a neutral
construction that leaves the result open, and forbids any wording of certainty or confirmation, even
inside the question.

The prompt digest adds the surface policy and renderer versions, so a change to either moves it.
