## DATASCAN_PORT_SET_IS_REGION_DEPENDENT

*Evidence:* Within the SAME category, the same table gives US/FR a list of 500 ports and SG/CN the TOP 25 ports.

The datascan port set is not one set. It is at least two, applied to different parts of the scanned population by different scanner regions.

**The construct counts distinct public IPv4 addresses answering on TCP/22 during a window. If port 22 is inside the 500 and outside the TOP 25, then an address reached only by the SG/CN rotation is not eligible to appear at all, while an address reached by US/FR is. The resulting count would mix two populations selected under two different configurations, and the difference would live inside the apparatus rather than between apparatuses.**

*It is not a refutation:* Whether 22 is in either list is unknown, so this is a named risk to the frame rather than an established defect. It bounds what a count from this apparatus could mean; it does not establish that the apparatus is wrong.

## Historical configuration drift

Basis: Retrospective 2024 and Roadmap 2025, published on the official ONYPHE blog.

- *From 200+ ports scanned to more than 400 ports;*
- *Bi-monthly refresh rate from FR and US countries, instead of once per month previously;*
- *More scanning from HK and SG locations (86 ports for SG & 42 for HK);*
- *New category of information: CTISCAN dedicated to Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI);*
- *Number of scanned ports: from 200+ to 1300+ per month*

Both the regions and their port counts differ between the two documents. Neither is wrong: they describe different times. What they establish together is that this configuration moves.

*Not a contradiction:* A retrospective describing 2024 and a current page describing now are not in conflict. Reading them as a contradiction would be the error; reading them as drift is what they are.

**APPARATUS_CONFIGURATION_MUST_BE_TIME_ADDRESSABLE was added in Mission 1.63 on the observation that neither apparatus binds its port list to a window. It is now demonstrated rather than anticipated: this configuration is published as having changed, in numbers, by the provider itself.**

## Configuration time-addressability

The retrospective dates aggregate CHANGES to a year. No first-party page publishes which ports were in the datascan set on a given date, and a narrative count of how many ports were scanned is not a membership list bound to a window.

**Before this mission, the port set was undated and might have been constant. It is now documented as having moved, so a count over a past window depends on a configuration whose value at that window is published nowhere.**

## Offered and not added: `APPARATUS_CONFIGURATION_MUST_BE_UNIFORM_ACROSS_THE_FRAME`

Where one apparatus applies different measurement configurations to different parts of its own population, a single count drawn from it mixes populations selected under different rules, and the apparatus must publish which configuration reached which part of the frame.

*Demonstrated by:* the same category scanning 500 ports from two regions and the TOP 25 from two others

*Why it is not already represented:* SAMPLING_IS_LOAD_BEARING compares population definitions BETWEEN apparatuses. THE_RETRIEVABLE_FRAME_IS_NOT_THE_MEASURED_FRAME governs what a requester may retrieve. APPARATUS_CONFIGURATION_MUST_BE_TIME_ADDRESSABLE governs WHEN a configuration applied. None governs heterogeneity WITHIN one apparatus at one time.

*Why it was not added here:* The registry is a frozen contract owned by another mission's renderer and tests, and §40 sets REGISTRY_UNCHANGED as the default. A documentation-reconciliation mission editing an apparatus contract in passing is the change-control shape docs/CLAUDE.md §Change control refuses. It is recorded here so a mission with the registry in scope can adopt or decline it on the evidence.
