## The structural finding

**A quantity that exists only because a platform recorded it can be measured only by that platform. A quantity that exists in the world independently of any measurer can be measured by more than one apparatus. Every source in this portfolio measures the first kind.**

Wikimedia's request counts exist because Wikimedia's servers logged them. Stack Overflow's question counts exist because Stack Overflow published them. GDELT's term frequencies exist because GDELT crawled a corpus and counted. TED's award totals exist because contracting authorities filed notices there. Each of these is the sole possible apparatus for its own quantity, and a second publication route is a second copy rather than a second measurement.

**It generalises.** Mission 1.46 found that for official statistics the measurement happens once, at the national producer, and the international publishers are distribution layers. This mission finds the same shape one domain over: for platform data the measurement happens once, at the platform, and every API, dump and dashboard is a distribution layer. The two findings are one finding about where measurement actually occurs.

**Consequence.** Corroboration and contradiction are structurally unavailable for platform-mediated evidence, and no amount of source acquisition changes that. What can be corroborated is a world quantity, and the portfolio currently holds exactly one family that names one -- national population -- which Mission 1.46 refuted on provenance.

*It is a search instruction. An independence-capable route must be sought among quantities that exist independently of their measurer, and this mission found one that qualifies on every epistemic gate.*
