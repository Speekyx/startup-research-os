## New requirements for the registry

### `ENUMERATED_EXCEPTIONS_MAKE_A_LINEAGE_CLAIM_CHECKABLE`

*From Mission 1.61.* An affirmative lineage statement is only exhaustive if its exceptions are enumerated and closed. 'We collect everything ourselves, and the only exceptions are X and Y' can be checked against the load-bearing predicate; 'our data comes from scans, sensors, blocklists and many other sources' cannot, because the list does not end. The second is LEVEL 1 however confidently it asserts its own scanning.

*Discovered how:* Both sides in one mission: the anchor supplied a closed clause and a partner candidate supplied an open one, and the difference is the whole distance between a checkable claim and an unfalsifiable one.

### `LINEAGE_EXHAUSTIVENESS_IS_NOT_FRAME_EXHAUSTIVENESS`

*From Mission 1.61.* That every record was produced by the apparatus's own probing says nothing about which addresses it reached. A7 asks who produced the observation; A5 asks which addresses were probed. A lineage sentence must never be cited for coverage, and an apparatus can satisfy A7 completely while probing an incomplete or sampled frame.

*Discovered how:* The anchor's lineage clause is the strongest in this arc and its frame is a sampled-or-census question its documentation does not answer. Both are true at once.
