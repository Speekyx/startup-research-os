## The two facts this mission keeps apart

| fact | means |
| --- | --- |
| approval | an operator said this exact action MAY be performed, naming it by a hash that was recomputed and matched |
| execution | somebody performed it |

They arrive close together, the second usually follows the first, and once the approval exists every field the execution record needs is already known -- recipient, subject, body, channel. A record could fill itself in completely and be entirely fictional.

**A permission is not an event. The gap between them is exactly where a log stops describing what happened and starts describing what was allowed.**

*And the new half:* For every earlier decision this repository has stopped at, the act happened INSIDE it and a guard could require a person. A manual outbound send happens outside it, so no guard can watch. The repository can record an attestation and must say that is what it is.
