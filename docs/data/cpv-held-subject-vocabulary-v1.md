## Present in the held population and outside the frozen universe

`9221`, `9232`, `9237` — the freeze took the codes that FORM a cohort under the production membership rule at grain 4, not every code PRESENT on a held notice. These three appear only on notices that also carry another class code, so under C4_REFUSE_AMBIGUOUS_NOTICE they form no cohort at grain 4 and can be no candidate at that grain they are outside the frozen universe, and the freeze is what stops a label from changing which codes get looked up. Widening it after labels had been read would be that move with a structural excuse.
